# Data Sources

## FFC Annual Integrated Report 2025
- **URL:** https://ffc.com.pk/investor-relations/reports
- **File:** Annual Integrated Report 2025 (downloaded as Financial-statements-2025.pdf)
- **Covers:** FY2025 and FY2024 (unconsolidated comparative figures)
- **Downloaded:** 2026-10-24
- **Pages used:**
  - p.305-306: Statement of Financial Position
  - p.308: Statement of Profit or Loss
  - p.310: Statement of Cash Flows
  - p.311: Statement of Changes in Equity

## FFC Annual Report 2024
- **URL:** https://ffc.com.pk/investor-relations/reports
- **Covers:** FY2024 and FY2023 (unconsolidated comparative figures) — used for FY2023 reference data only
- **Pages used:**
  - p.268: Statement of Financial Position
  - p.270: Statement of Profit or Loss
  - p.272: Statement of Cash Flows

## Notes
- Both reports' unconsolidated statements used throughout. Consolidated Financial Statements also exist in the 2025 report (starting p.426) but not yet used.
- FY2024 figures differ slightly between the two reports (restated between publications) — see PROJECT_JOURNAL.md for the specific discrepancy found in "Other investments – net" (investing cash flow line).
- Large source PDFs are not committed to this repo (see .gitignore) — re-download from the URL above if needed.
